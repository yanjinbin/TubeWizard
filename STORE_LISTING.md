## English

### Name
TubeWizard — Single Playback, Auto HD & More

### Summary (short, ≤132 chars)
Play only one YouTube tab at a time, force HD/FPS, block Picture-in-Picture, theater mode, autoplay control & a buffer/speed HUD.

### Detailed description
TubeWizard bundles the player tweaks power users actually want — all local, all private, no account needed.

★ Single Playback
Only one YouTube video plays across all your tabs. Open videos in new background
tabs without interrupting what you're watching. A video takes over only when you
actively click play; switching to a non-YouTube tab keeps your current video going.

★ Auto HD + FPS
Automatically set your preferred resolution (up to 8K/4320p) and frame rate on
every video, with optional fallback to the next best quality when your choice
isn't available.

★ Disable Picture-in-Picture
Block the PiP button and the PiP API so videos never pop out unexpectedly.

★ Theater Mode by default
Always start videos in the wide theater layout.

★ Autoplay control
Mirror YouTube's "autoplay next" toggle to your preference, applied on every page.

★ Hide Continue Watching
Remove the continue-watching mini-player popup that appears in the bottom-right
corner when navigating away from a video.

★ Buffer + Speed HUD
An optional readout in the player control bar showing live buffer health and
download speed — zero extra network usage, it just reads what your browser
already knows.

Privacy first: no data collection, no tracking, no external servers. Your
settings stay in your browser.

TubeWizard is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by,
or sponsored by YouTube or Google LLC.

## 简体中文

### 名称
TubeWizard — 单一播放、自动高清等

### 简短描述(≤132 字符）
全局只播放一个 YouTube 标签、强制高清/帧率、禁用画中画、影院模式、自动播放控制,以及缓冲/速度 HUD。

### 详细描述
TubeWizard 集合了重度用户真正需要的 YouTube 播放器增强功能——全部本地运行、注重隐私、无需登录。

★ 单一播放
所有标签页中只播放一个 YouTube 视频。在后台新标签打开视频时不会打断你正在观看的内容；只有当你主动点击播放时，新视频才会接管播放权；切换到非 YouTube 标签则继续播放原视频。

★ 自动高清 + 帧率
每个视频自动设置你偏好的分辨率（最高 8K/4320p）和帧率，并可在所选画质不可用时自动降级到次优画质。

★ 禁用画中画
拦截画中画按钮和画中画 API，视频不会意外弹出小窗。

★ 默认影院模式
始终以宽屏影院布局开始播放。

★ 自动播放控制
将 YouTube 的「自动播放下一个」开关同步为你的偏好，每个页面自动应用。

★ 隐藏继续观看
移除离开视频页面后右下角弹出的「继续观看」浮窗。

★ 缓冲 + 速度 HUD
在播放控制栏内可选显示实时缓冲健康度和下载速度——零额外网络开销，只读取浏览器已有的数据。

隐私至上：不收集任何数据、不追踪、无外部服务器。你的设置只保存在浏览器本地。

TubeWizard 是独立扩展，与 YouTube 或 Google LLC 无任何关联，也未获其认可或赞助。

## Privacy practices (Dashboard form)

- **Single purpose**: Enhance the YouTube playback experience (playback
  coordination, quality, layout, and an optional stats overlay).
- **Permission justifications**:
  - `tabs`: Detect and pause other open YouTube tabs to enforce single playback.
  - `storage`: Persist the user's feature settings locally.
  - Host `*.youtube.com`: Inject the feature scripts on YouTube pages only.
- **Data usage**: Check "I do not collect or use user data." No data is
  collected, transmitted, or sold.
- **Privacy policy URL**: link to your hosted PRIVACY.md (e.g. a GitHub raw or
  Pages URL).
